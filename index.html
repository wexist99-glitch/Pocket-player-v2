    Add music

    Pick files from your phone, or paste a direct link to a file.

    Direct file links only (ending in .mp3, .mp4 and so on). Only download files you have the right to use. Links to streaming or video sites will not work.

    Songs are stored inside this app and never uploaded. Music video files (such as .mp4) work too: tap Show video while one is playing. You can add hundreds at once; they are saved one at a time, so keep the app open until the count finishes. Songs stay until you tap × to remove them or clear this app's data in Android settings.

          Options

          Cover art

          Pocket Player reads cover art already embedded in your MP3, M4A and FLAC files automatically, with no internet needed.

          When on, songs with no embedded art can be matched against Apple's iTunes song catalog by title and artist to find a matching cover. This sends those song and artist names to Apple over the internet. Nothing else about your library is sent, and no audio is uploaded.

          Backup

          Save all your songs, favorites and stats to one file, or restore from one. Keep this file somewhere safe, like a computer or cloud storage.
